MP3 Content Pipeline

A private, internal tool. Not a public service.

MP3 Content Pipeline is used only by its owner to publish and manage videos on the YouTube channels he operates. It connects to YouTube through Google's official YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, and only ever acts on channels whose owner has signed in and approved it.

What it does

  • Uploads videos to the owner's own channels and sets their titles, descriptions and tags.
  • Reads view and performance figures for those same channels, to decide what to post next.
  • Reads and replies to comments on the owner's own videos.

Who can use it

Nobody outside the owner's own Google accounts. There is no sign-up, no customer account, and no data from anyone else's channel.

How information from Google is handled is set out in the privacy policy.
