Our purpose

We started Buyma because we were tired of counterfeits and overpriced books.

Buying online had started to feel like a gamble. Too often the “deal” turned out to be a knockoff, and the books we needed cost far more than they should have. We figured we weren't the only ones.

So we built the store we wanted to buy from: only genuine products, bought from established distributors with the invoice to prove it, priced fairly, and backed by real people. That's still the whole idea.

Authentic, always

Every product is bought from an authorized distributor, and we keep the invoice. No knockoffs, ever.

Fair prices

Our prices are checked against the market every day, so you get a fair deal without having to hunt for one.

Real people

Tracked shipping on every order, and every message answered by our team, not a bot.
