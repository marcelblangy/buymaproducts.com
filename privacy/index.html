Privacy Policy

MP3 Content Pipeline · Effective October 5, 2026

This policy covers MP3 Content Pipeline (“the app”), a private, internal tool operated by Buyma Products LLC (“we”). The app is used only by its owner, on YouTube channels he operates. It is not offered to the public.

Information the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google and approves access, the app can use these Google permissions, on his own channels only:

  • YouTube account details (channel name and ID), to confirm it is acting on the right channel.
  • Upload and manage videos, to publish the owner's videos and set their titles, descriptions and tags.
  • Read and manage comments on the owner's videos, to reply to viewers.
  • YouTube Analytics (read-only), to see how the owner's videos perform.

How it is used

Only to publish and manage the owner's own videos and to measure how they perform. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone.

Where it is stored

Google's access tokens are stored on the owner's own computer. There is no server database and no copy held by any third party.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page. Deleting the stored token from the owner's computer also ends the app's access.

Google and YouTube

MP3 Content Pipeline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
